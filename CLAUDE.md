# LUMORA: Project Context for Claude

> **This document is your briefing. It explains what Lumora is, how it's organized, and how to work with it.**

---

## Quick Summary

**Lumora** is a hard-magic fantasy novel universe built on a simple principle:

> *No solution without foundation. No effect without cause. No power without limit.*

The story takes place on a planet called **Lumora** where a 5th fundamental force — **Noetic Force (N-Force)** — allows conscious beings to influence physical reality through thought, emotion, and intention.

It's not arbitrary magic. It's an extended physics system where consciousness is real.

---

## Core Philosophy

This project exists because the creator is frustrated with fantasy worlds that don't take their own rules seriously.

**Lumora's Core Promise:**
- Magic has consistent rules
- Problems can't be solved by ignoring the system
- Power is meaningful because it's limited
- Every solution must be earned, not gifted

**Key Principle from the Manifest:**
> *No Deus ex Machina. No arbitrary abilities. No plot magic. No shortcuts.*

## The Noetic System (Magic System)

### Three Core Components

#### 1. Noetic Force (N-Force)
The **5th fundamental force of physics** (alongside gravity, EM, weak, strong).
- Enables consciousness to influence physical reality indirectly
- Not "magic" — it's an expanded natural law
- Exists everywhere, but varies in strength by region

#### 2. Noetic Field (N-Field)
The **medium** through which Noetic Energy moves.
- Exists throughout the universe
- Enables transmission of noetic processes across space
- Strength varies: **Hotspots** (strong) vs. **noetically dead zones** (weak)

#### 3. Noetic Energy
The **active component** that does the work.
- Generated by living beings (thoughts, emotions, intentions)
- Carries **information** (not just force)
- Requires **conscious intent** to be shaped precisely
- Can be intuitive (unprecise) or deliberate (controlled)

### The Basic Process

```
Consciousness → Noetic Energy Generation → Movement through N-Field → 
Interaction with N-Force → Physical Effect
```

### Key Rules

✅ **Always true:**
- Energy must be generated by a conscious being
- Effects must follow physical laws (can't create energy, only redirect it)
- Precision requires knowledge and control
- Intuitive effects work but are unpredictable
- Some materials (silicon crystals) amplify noetic processes

❌ **Never true:**
- Noetic effects are unlimited
- Magic solves any problem
- You can break physics, only bend it
- Willpower alone is enough (structure matters)
- Effects are instant (they take time and energy)

### Common Misconceptions to Avoid

1. **Noetik ≠ telepathy** (though noetic perception is real)
2. **All beings can use it, but not equally** (sensitive perception requires specific biology)
3. **It's not a power system for chosen ones** (it's a natural biology for all)
4. **Knowledge ≠ power** (but knowledge makes power precise)

## The World (Lumora)

### Planet Basics
- **Name:** Lumora
- **Sun:** Orthyros (star the planet orbits)
- **Orbital mechanics:** Elliptical orbit creates **Lighttides** and **Darktides** (periapsis/apoapsis points where day/night are extreme)
- **Special events:** Dunkeltag (day-long darkness) and Lichttag (day-long light) are religious holidays for many peoples

### The Peoples (Sapient Races)

10+ distinct humanoid species, each with different relationships to Noetik:

| Race | Archetype | Noetic Affinity |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Aviari** | Bird-humanoid | Sky/wind intuition |
| **Chronari** | Humanoid | Strong time-sense |
| **Dragons** | Western dragons | Power & presence |
| **Eldari** | Elf-like | Natural affinity |
| **Golethari** | Silicon-based | Crystalline resonance |
| **Kharell** | Reptile-humanoid | Cold rationality |
| **Leviathari** | Water-dwelling | Fluid adaptation |
| **Luminaari** | Fey-like | Intuitive sensitivity |
| **Menschen** | Human-like | Balanced versatility |
| **Sylvari** | Plant-humanoid | Growth & time |
| **Theriani** | Beast-humanoid | Emotional intensity |
| **Vulkani** | Dwarf-like | Structure & craft |

**Note:** No race is "chosen" or inherently superior. Differences are biological, not hierarchical.

### Material Properties

**Noetically Active Materials:**
- Silicon-based crystals (excellent storage/conductors)
- Specific liquids (rare)
- Certain plant compounds
- These enable technology, rituals, and amplification

## Working Principles

### For the Creator (You)

1. **Refer back to the Manifest** when stuck
   - Does this solution follow the rules?
   - Am I taking the easiest way or the right way?
   - Is my system consistent?

2. **The Kern is sacred**
   - Don't change core philosophy to fix story problems
   - Fix the story instead

3. **Detail grows organically**
   - You don't need every region mapped before writing
   - You don't need every character before plot
   - But you need the system solid

- Anything that contradicts the Manifest

- Propose arbitrary powers
- Create characters with abilities outside the system
- Write scenes that violate the Noetic rules
